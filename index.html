The Ritual of Scent

The 5 Sacred Attar Application Rituals

Unlike alcohol-based spray perfumes that dissipate quickly into the air, pure attar oils are intimate, heat-activated, and bloom slowly over many hours across specific heat and fiber zones.

01

Inner Wrists

The Radial Pulse Point

Roll the applicator gently onto your inner wrists. Allow body warmth to awaken the delicate top notes. Strict master rule: Never rub wrists together—frictional shear crushes and bruises fragile top notes (rose, jasmine, citrus), cutting longevity in half.

✋ Radial Artery 🚫 Never Rub (Preserves Notes)
✦ Tap to Reveal Master Science ▸
02

Neck & Behind Ears

Intimate Convection Sillage

Touch the applicator behind your earlobes and along the carotid pulse point. Because warm air naturally rises, it creates a personal convection chimney—diffusing an intimate 2–3 foot scent aura whenever you speak, turn your head, or move.

👂 Carotid Pulse 💨 Upward Convection Aura
✦ Tap to Reveal Master Science ▸
03

Inner Elbows

Protected Warmth Trap

The inner elbow fold (antecubital fossa) is a natural body-heat pocket shielded from open air and washing. Applying here anchors heavier heart & base notes (oud, amber, resins) for sustained, continuous 14+ hour diffusion.

💪 Antecubital Fold ⏳ Anchors Oud & Resins
✦ Tap to Reveal Master Science ▸
04

Beard & Hair

Palm-Warmed Conditioning Aura

With 0% alcohol, pure attar nourishes hair cuticles naturally without drying. Critical technique: Never apply heavy oil directly to beard roots. Warm 1 drop between your palms for 3 seconds, then lightly stroke through the outer beard and hair ends for a 24–48h aura.

🧔 0% Alcohol (Non-Drying) 🤲 Warm in Palms First
✦ Tap to Reveal Master Science ▸
05

Collars & "Faha" Secret

72H+ Longevity Without Staining

Natural fabrics (cotton, linen, wool) hold pure oils for 72+ hours. Critical caution: Dark botanical oils can stain white silks. Apply strictly to inner collar folds or cuff seams—or use the royal Awadhi Faha (फाहा) method: tucking an attar-scented wisp of raw cotton into an inner pocket.

👔 Inner Collar Seams 🪙 Awadhi Cotton Faha (फाहा)
