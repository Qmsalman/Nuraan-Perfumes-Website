The Ritual of Scent

How to Apply Pure Concentrated Attar

Unlike alcohol-based spray perfumes that dissipate quickly into the air, pure attar oils are intimate, heat-activated, and bloom slowly over many hours.

01

Glide Over Warm Pulse Points

Roll the applicator gently over the wrists, inner elbows, and the sides of your neck. Do not vigorously rub the oil—allow your natural body warmth to awaken the top and heart notes.

02

Beard & Hair Scenting

Pure alcohol-free oils condition hair and beards naturally. Roll a drop onto your palms, rub gently together, and stroke lightly through your beard or hair ends for a lingering aura that lasts all day.

03

Fabric Longevity (3+ Days)

Because our attars contain zero harsh chemical solvents, applying a light glide to the collar or cuffs of cotton, wool, or linen garments allows the base notes of oud and amber to persist for 48 to 72 hours.
